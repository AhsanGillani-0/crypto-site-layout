Understanding Local Sarafa Rates

In Pakistan, major Sarafa Associations in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad announce daily rates based on international London Bullion Market (LBMA) spot prices combined with the open-market rupee parity.

Physical gold jewelry prices include additional "Making Charges" (Katai / Mazdoori) which typically range from 5% to 15% depending on artisan complexity.

Gold FAQs

What is the difference between 24K and 22K gold?

24 Karat is 99.9% pure gold, primarily used for investment bars and biscuits. 22 Karat contains 91.6% gold alloyed with silver or copper for structural durability in jewelry.

Why does PAXG track spot gold?

PAX Gold (PAXG) is a digital token where each unit is 1:1 physically backed by one fine troy ounce of London Good Delivery gold stored in Brink's vaults.
