Frequently Asked Questions

Methodologies, formulas, and data origin behind our calculators

How are Pakistani Gold per Tola rates calculated? ▼

1 Pakistani Tola equals exactly 11.6638 grams. 1 Troy Ounce (the international gold standard) equals 31.1035 grams. The formula calculates: (Gold Spot USD × USD/PKR Rate × 11.6638) / 31.1035. Spot gold is pulled live from PAXG token (1:1 backed by physical gold) and paired with interbank USD/PKR rates. Local Sarafa associations may include small regional import premiums.

Why is Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) effective during volatile drawdowns? ▼

DCA eliminates the emotional hazard of attempting to time market cycle bottoms. By allocating a fixed fiat amount at regular intervals, an investor acquires more units when prices fall and fewer when prices rise, systematically reducing the aggregate average cost per coin.

What is the standard capital risk rule in the Position Size Calculator? ▼

Institutional risk managers strongly advise risking no more than 1% to 2% of total capital on any single setup. Position sizing adjusts quantity inversely to stop-loss distance: tighter invalidation allows larger notional size while keeping dollar loss capped.

Are the data sources genuinely live or hardcoded? ▼

All prices on MarketPulse stream directly via keyless public REST endpoints from Binance Vision, Alternative.me, and global currency repositories. Data is refreshed and cached in-memory for 60 seconds to optimize performance without layout shift.
