How Dollar-Cost Averaging Works

Dollar-cost averaging is an institutional risk-mitigation framework wherein equal dollar increments are allocated systematically into an asset at regular intervals regardless of current spot market fluctuations.

  • Systematically mitigates timing risk during macro consolidation phases.
  • Acquires more units during oversold troughs and fewer during overbought peaks.
  • Replaces emotional fear-of-missing-out (FOMO) with disciplined execution.

DCA Strategy FAQs

Is weekly DCA superior to monthly DCA?

Historical simulations reveal marginal difference in aggregate ROI between weekly and monthly intervals for long-term horizons, but weekly schedules reduce local variance.

How are Binance klines fetched?

The calculator queries the public Binance Vision REST API endpoints for exact historical daily closes across the selected duration.
