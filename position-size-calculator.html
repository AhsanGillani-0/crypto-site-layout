Position Sizing Guidelines

What is the mathematical position size formula?

Position Size = (Total Capital × Risk %) / |Entry Price - Stop Loss Price|. This guarantees that if the stop-loss is hit, the total loss equals exactly the defined risk dollar limit.

Why is a Risk/Reward ratio over 1:2 recommended?

With a 1:2 R:R, a trader needs only a 35% win rate to maintain long-term profitability, absorbing consecutive losses while protecting portfolio expectancy.
