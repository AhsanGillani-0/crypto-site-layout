Contrarian Rule: "Be fearful when others are greedy, and greedy when others are fearful." Extreme sentiment historically marks tactical accumulation or distribution zones.

Index Construction Factors

  • Volatility (25%): Measures current market volatility compared to 30-day and 90-day moving averages.
  • Market Volume & Momentum (25%): Compares current trading volume against historic baseline levels.
  • Social Media Sentiment (15%): Scrapes Twitter and Reddit discussions and engagement velocity.
  • Bitcoin Dominance (10%): Rise in BTC dominance typically reflects retail risk-off flight to safety.

Sentiment FAQs

How often does the index update?

Alternative.me updates the composite Fear & Greed index once every 24 hours at 00:00 UTC.

Does Extreme Fear mean a bottom is in?

Not necessarily. Markets can remain in Extreme Fear for weeks during protracted bear phases, but it signals heightened retail capitulation.
