PnL Calculation FAQs

How is the break-even sell price determined?

Break-even accounts for both the initial buy fee and the exit sell fee: Break-even = Buy Price × (1 + Fee) / (1 - Fee).

What is the difference between Maker and Taker fees?

Maker orders add liquidity to the order book (limit orders) and usually enjoy discounted fees. Taker orders remove liquidity (market orders) and incur the standard exchange fee schedule.
